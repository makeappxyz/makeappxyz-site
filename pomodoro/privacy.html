Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2025

1. Information We Collect

Flight Focus is designed with privacy first:

• No personal data collection or tracking
• No analytics or user behavior monitoring
• No user accounts or registration required
• All flight history stored locally on your device only
• Location access only when you choose "Use Current Location"

2. Location Services

Location access is completely optional:

• Only requested when you tap "Use Current Location"
• Used solely to find nearest airports
• Never stored or transmitted to any servers
• You can always select airports manually
• iOS "While Using App" permission - never tracks in background

3. Screen Time Data Usage

Flight Focus uses Apple's Screen Time API to help you focus:

• Allows you to temporarily block selected apps during focus sessions
• Shows list of installed apps on your device for selection
• All Screen Time data is encrypted and inaccessible to us
• We don't collect, store, or transmit any app usage information
• Blocking functionality runs entirely on your device
• You control which apps to block for each session

Important: The Screen Time API is a system-level Apple feature. We cannot see your app usage statistics, browsing history, or any other Screen Time data. We only request permission to temporarily restrict apps you explicitly select.

4. Data Storage

All your data stays on your device:

• Focus sessions saved locally using Core Data
• Flight history stored in device's private documents
• Settings and preferences in UserDefaults
• Optional iCloud sync for your data across your devices
• No external servers or databases

5. iCloud Sync

Optional sync across your Apple devices:

• Uses your personal iCloud account
• Data encrypted by Apple's iCloud security
• Only syncs between your own devices
• Can be disabled anytime in Settings
• We have no access to your iCloud data

6. Your Choices

You have full control over your data:

• Disable iCloud sync at any time
• Delete app data from Settings
• Deny location permission and select airports manually
• Choose which apps to block (or none at all)
• Delete the app to remove all local data

7. Third-Party Services

Flight Focus uses these Apple services only:

• Apple StoreKit: For premium features and subscriptions
• Apple CloudKit: For optional iCloud sync
• Apple Screen Time API: For app blocking during focus sessions
• No advertising networks
• No analytics or tracking services
• No third-party payment processors

8. Children's Privacy

Flight Focus is safe for all ages:

• No data collection from anyone, including children
• No social features or user interaction
• No external communication
• Family-friendly focus timer
• Suitable for educational use

9. Policy Changes

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

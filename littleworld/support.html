How do I restore Plus?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase can be restored; consumable cans cannot.

How do I cancel or request a refund?

Manage subscriptions in system Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Apple handles refund requests at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens when Plus ends?

Your collected postcards, saved photos and letters remain available to view, share and export. Your selected portrait and signature remain. Alarms stay free.

Do I need to grant every permission?

No. Permissions are requested for the feature you choose. Without location access, weather is simulated. Without voice access, you can use touch. Screen Time, reminders and alarms are optional.

How does iCloud work?

Plus can sync your world and photos with your private iCloud. Turn it on in Settings → iCloud Sync. Turning sync off or uninstalling does not erase existing cloud backups. Screen Time tokens and permissions stay on the device.

Something went wrong. What should I send?

Email your app version, device model, system version and steps to reproduce. Screenshots and diagnostic reports are optional; remove personal information before sharing them.

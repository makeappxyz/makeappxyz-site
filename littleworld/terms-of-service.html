What this is

Little World is a personal companion app for individual, noncommercial use. It keeps you company while you give your attention a place to rest; it does not grade you.

What it is not

This is not a productivity assessment tool or a medical, psychological, or sleep-monitoring product. Sleep journeys record only the duration you enter, with no physiological monitoring. Do not use it where precise timing or safety-critical reliability is required.

Subscriptions and purchases

A Little While+ offers monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase, all with the same features.

Plus includes access to collect special postcards by completing journeys, continued instant photography, weekly cat letters, app blocking without a daily limit and exceptions, more built-in portraits, iCloud sync, automatic backups and restoring the latest backup.

Subscriptions renew automatically. Cancel anytime in system Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions; access continues until the current period ends. Subscriptions and lifetime purchases can be recovered on a new device using Restore Purchases. Apple handles refunds under App Store policy. Cans are consumable gifts and cannot be restored.

Companionship, journeys, collecting basic postcards, and handwritten signature creation and editing are free, along with 3 successfully saved trial photos (deleting photos does not restore the allowance) 5 weekly letters and translation of 3 letters. App blocking is free for 3 uses per day.

Collected postcards, photos and saved letters remain available to view, share and export normally. Expiry does not remove your selected portrait or signature; turning off app blocking and creating and managing alarms remain free.

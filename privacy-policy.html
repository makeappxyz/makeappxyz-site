Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to all applications developed and published by MakeXYZ, including but not limited to Sudoku, Flight Focus, EchoPaper, KiddoArt, Linguist, and BitZen. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Our Privacy Principles

We build our apps with privacy as a core principle:

  • Local-First: Most of our apps process data entirely on your device
  • Minimal Collection: We only collect data that is essential for app functionality
  • No Tracking: We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs
  • User Control: You can delete your data at any time

Data Collection

The majority of our apps do not collect any personal information. Data you create within our apps (such as game progress, notes, drawings, or translations) is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Some apps may offer optional iCloud sync, which uses Apple's encrypted cloud storage. In these cases, data is synced to your personal iCloud account and is not accessible to us.

On-Device Processing

Apps that use AI features (such as EchoPaper and Linguist) perform all processing locally on your device. Your voice recordings, text, and translations never leave your device and are never sent to external servers.

Apple Health Integration

Some apps (such as BitZen) may integrate with Apple Health to record mindfulness or activity data. This integration is optional and only records duration or activity metrics - never the content of your sessions. Health data is stored in your personal Apple Health container and protected by Apple's privacy policies.

No Account Required

Our apps do not require account creation or sign-up. There is no login system and therefore no personal information such as email addresses or names is collected.

Children's Privacy

Apps designed for children (such as KiddoArt) are built with extra care for privacy. They do not collect any personal information, do not include social features, and do not display advertisements.

Third-Party Services

Our apps do not include third-party analytics, crash reporting, or advertising services. We have no way to track how you use our apps or what content you create.

Data Deletion

Since your data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting the app or using the app's built-in data management features. When you delete an app, all associated data is permanently removed.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated revision date.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2025

Data Collection

EchoPaper prioritizes your privacy. All voice recordings and transcriptions are processed locally on your device by default. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to our servers.

Local Processing

Voice-to-text conversion uses WhisperKit - OpenAI's Whisper model optimized for Apple Silicon. Your audio never leaves your device. All AI processing is performed locally using Apple Intelligence models.

Complete Privacy

EchoPaper uses only on-device AI models for complete privacy. There are no external API dependencies, and network AI features are permanently disabled. Your data stays entirely on your device.

iCloud Sync

When iCloud sync is enabled, your notes are encrypted and stored in your private iCloud container. Only you can access this data with your Apple ID.

Permissions

EchoPaper requests microphone access for recording audio. This permission is required for core functionality. We also request optional permissions for Reminders and Calendar integration.

No Third-Party Services

Unlike other voice note apps, EchoPaper does not use any cloud AI services. All processing happens on your device using Apple's latest on-device AI technology.

Privacy policy

For the Peel iPhone app.

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Summary

Peel makes and keeps your photos on your iPhone. Peel has no account and no server. It shows no advertisements and sells no data. We do not collect personal data.

Camera and microphone

The camera works only while the camera screen is open. The microphone records the sound of Live Photos. The picture and the sound go only into your photos.

Photos

Peel adds a photo to Photos when you save it, or when “Save to Photos” is on. It asks only to add photos. When you make a sticker from a photo, you pick it in the system photo picker, and Peel receives only that photo.

Faces and people

Peel finds faces and people on your iPhone, only to place stickers and backdrops. The detection runs on the device. Nothing about faces or people is kept or sent.

Your stickers

Stickers that you make stay in the app on your iPhone. If you share or copy a sticker, it goes only where you send it. If you delete the app, your stickers are deleted.

Purchases

Peel offers one optional in-app purchase, Sticker Maker. Apple processes the payment through the App Store. We receive no payment details. The app checks the purchase on your iPhone with StoreKit.

iPhone Duo

On iPhone Duo, the outer display can show an animation and the picture of the camera to the people in front of it. This happens on the device only.

Feedback

If you send feedback by email, your email address and your message come to us. We use them only to answer you. For privacy questions or deletion requests, write to sudoku42game@gmail.com.

Children

Peel does not collect personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version shows on this page with a new date.

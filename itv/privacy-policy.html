iTV

Privacy policy

For the iTV iPhone and iPad app.

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Summary

iTV plays the TV playlists that you add. iTV has no account and shows no advertisements. We do not sell data, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Your library

Your sources, channels, favorites, names, hidden channels, recently watched list, programme guide and settings stay on your device. Passwords for sources are kept in the iOS Keychain. If you delete the app, this data is deleted.

iCloud Sync

If you turn on iCloud Sync, iTV keeps your sources, favorites and names in your private iCloud database, so they are the same on your devices. Apple keeps this data in your Apple Account. We cannot read it.

Streams and guides

iTV connects directly to the playlist, stream and guide addresses that you add. Those providers receive your IP address and the requests that your device sends, as with any web request. Their own privacy policies apply. iTV does not include, sell or check channels.

Purchases

iTV Plus is optional. Apple processes the payment through the App Store, and we receive no payment details. Our purchase service provider, RevenueCat, receives an anonymous app identifier, your purchase history and your subscription status. It uses them to confirm and restore your purchases.

Reminders

When you set a programme reminder, iTV schedules a local notification on your device. No server sends it, and no push token leaves your device.

App lock

If you turn on the app lock, iOS checks your Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. iTV receives only the result.

Export and delete

In Settings, Export Library makes a file of your library that you can keep. Delete All Data removes your library from the device and from your iCloud.

Diagnostic logs

iTV keeps a short log of what it did, such as how long a channel took to open, on your device. The log contains no stream addresses, channel names or passwords. It leaves your device only if you attach it to a feedback email yourself.

Feedback

If you send feedback by email, your address, your message and anything you attach come to us. We use them only to answer you. For privacy questions, write to itv.feedback@gmail.com.

Children

iTV does not collect personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version shows on this page with a new date.

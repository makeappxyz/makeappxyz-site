iTV

Privacy policy

For the iTV iPhone and iPad app.

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Summary

iTV plays the TV playlists that you add. iTV has no account. It shows no advertisements, has no analytics and sells no data. We do not collect personal data.

Your library

Your sources, channels, favorites, names, hidden channels, recently watched list, programme guide and settings stay on your device. Passwords for sources are kept in the iOS Keychain. If you delete the app, this data is deleted.

iCloud Sync

If you turn on iCloud Sync, iTV keeps your sources, favorites and names in your private iCloud database, so they are the same on your devices. Apple keeps this data in your Apple Account. We cannot read it.

Streams and guides

iTV connects directly to the playlist, stream and guide addresses that you add. Those providers receive your IP address and the requests that your device sends, as with any web request. Their own privacy policies apply. iTV does not include, sell or check channels.

Purchases

iTV Plus is an optional subscription. Apple processes the payment through the App Store. We receive no payment details. To answer support questions, our server can receive Apple’s signed notices about subscriptions, such as a renewal or a refund. These contain a transaction ID and a product ID, not your name or payment details.

App lock

If you turn on the app lock, iOS checks your Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. iTV receives only the result.

Export and delete

In Settings, Export Library makes a file of your library that you can keep. Delete All Data removes your library from the device and from your iCloud.

Feedback

If you send feedback by email, your email address and your message come to us. We use them only to answer you. For privacy questions, write to tsuji.hiromoto@gmail.com.

Children

iTV does not collect personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version shows on this page with a new date.

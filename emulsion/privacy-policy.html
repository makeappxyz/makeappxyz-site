Emulsion

Privacy policy

For the Emulsion iPhone app.

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Summary

Emulsion keeps your photos on your iPhone. We do not collect your photos, your location, or your contacts. We do not show advertisements. We do not sell data.

Photos that you take

The app develops each photo on your iPhone. It keeps the developed photo in the app folder. If you turn on "Save to Photos", the app also adds a copy to your photo library. The app asks for add-only access, so it cannot see the other photos in your library.

Photos that you import

When you develop a photo from your library, you select it in the system photo picker. The app receives only the photo that you select. It does not see the other photos.

Camera

The app uses the camera only when the camera screen is open. The camera image goes to the screen and to the photo. It does not go to a server. A Live Photo movie stays on your iPhone, as the photo does.

Scene detection

The composition coach finds the scene, the subject, and the horizon in the camera image. This analysis runs on your iPhone. No image and no result leaves your iPhone.

Purchases

Apple processes the Club subscription through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. The app asks the App Store if your subscription is active.

Notifications

If you turn on the Daily Roll reminder, the app schedules local notifications on your iPhone. No server sends them.

Data that leaves your iPhone

None, except when you share a photo yourself, or when the App Store checks your subscription.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from any person, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version shows on this page with a new date.

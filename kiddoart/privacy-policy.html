🔒 KiddoArt

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

🌈 At KiddoArt, we believe that privacy is super important, especially for kids! We're committed to protecting your child's privacy and keeping their creative work safe. This policy explains how we handle data in our app - in simple, easy-to-understand terms!

📝 What We Collect

KiddoArt stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Artworks: All the wonderful drawings your child creates
  • Stories: Generated stories and their beautiful illustrations
  • Settings: App preferences and age settings (for age-appropriate content)
  • iCloud Sync (Optional): If you enable it, stories are synced to YOUR personal iCloud account. Only YOU have access to this data - we can't see it!

🚫 What We Do NOT Collect

We promise:

  • We do NOT collect any personal information
  • We do NOT track your child's behavior
  • We do NOT use analytics or tracking services
  • We do NOT share data with anyone
  • We do NOT show advertisements - EVER!

🔐 How We Keep Data Safe

  • All data stays on YOUR device - we never upload it to our servers
  • Optional iCloud sync uses Apple's super-secure iCloud system
  • We don't operate any servers or databases
  • We can't access your child's creations or data - they're all yours!

👶 Children's Privacy

KiddoArt is designed for children ages 3-12 and fully complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act):

  • No personal information is collected from children
  • All content creation happens locally on the device
  • Parents have full control over all data through device settings
  • No data is ever shared with third parties

🤖 AI Features

Our AI-powered story creation and illustration features work completely offline:

  • Story generation uses on-device Apple Intelligence (iOS 18.2+)
  • Image generation uses on-device Image Playground API (iOS 18.2+)
  • No data is sent to external servers for AI processing
  • All AI magic happens right on your device!

💪 Your Rights & Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Delete any artwork or story anytime within the app
  • Turn off iCloud sync in Settings if you don't want it
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data
  • You're always in charge of your data!

📬 Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we protect your child's privacy, we'd love to hear from you!

Email: support@kiddoart.app

✨ In Short: We believe in privacy by design. Your child's creativity stays on YOUR devices, and we NEVER see or access any of it. Their art is their own - always! 🎨❤️
